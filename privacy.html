Sharp Eye · Detective Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Sharp Eye mobile game handles information. Sharp Eye does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, photos, contacts, or location to play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves gameplay progress and preferences on your device. This can include completed cases and scores, stars, daily streak, sound and haptics settings, case unlock status, and cached case lists and puzzle images. The game does not send this gameplay information to a server operated by the Sharp Eye developer.

This information remains in the app's private storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes locally saved progress and downloaded case files.

Ads and Google Mobile Ads

Sharp Eye includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK for rewarded and interstitial advertising. Development builds use Google's test ad units. When ads are requested, Google may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google handles this information under its own policies.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform may show a consent message before ads are requested. You can also manage or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and its Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

Case content and GitHub Pages

The game connects to GitHub Pages to check for published case lists and controls and to download case images. These requests disclose your IP address and standard connection information to GitHub so it can deliver the requested files. GitHub handles server logs and related information under its Privacy Statement. Your scores, settings, and other gameplay progress are not included in these requests.

Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information or maintain a player account database. Information is processed by Google for advertising as described above and by GitHub as the hosting provider for case content. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

Security and retention

Case content is requested over HTTPS. Gameplay progress is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Google and GitHub determine their own retention periods for information they process; see their privacy policies for details.

Children

Sharp Eye does not require players to submit personal information to play. If you have a concern about information associated with a child, contact the developer using the support contact below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this webpage, with its revision date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Sharp Eye Google Play listing.
